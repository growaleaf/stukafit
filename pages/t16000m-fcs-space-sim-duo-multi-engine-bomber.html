What this counts

"Core functions" are counted from a dated primary source per aircraft class — not the game's full bindable-key list. "Slots" means physical buttons, axes, and hats on the controller, counted from the manufacturer's own product page. One physical input covers one function.

This tool reports counts. It does not tell you a controller is required, sufficient, or the best choice for IL-2 — that judgment is yours.
